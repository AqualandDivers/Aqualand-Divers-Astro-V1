<h2>
Discover Scuba Diving
</h2>

<p>
Want to try scuba diving before jumping all the way in? <br>
Try Discover Scuba Diving to experience what it's like to become a diver. <br>
Only thing you need to bring is yourself! We will go through all the skills you need, and answer any questions you have.

</p>

<h2>
Bubblemaker (Younger Divers)
</h2>

<p>
Younger divers can try the Bubblemaker experience! <br>
Children can experience what it's like to be a diver, and learn how to use equipment properly, under the supervision of our PADI professionals.
</p>

<h2>
Looking to go further
</h2>
Are you ready to jump all the way in? <br>
Get started with PADI Open Water and become a qualified scuba diver. <br>
PADI Open Water is the most popular and recognised scuba course in the world, through the course you'll learn everything you need to become an amazing diver, with the help of our PADI professionals.
